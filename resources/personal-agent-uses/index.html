The seven uses

In the order they appear in the video, each with its source where one exists.

Use 1

House Hunting on Autopilot

Koki's own case. She gave Muse and Grok Bot her requirements; they searched California listing sites for places that fit. Where they could respond to a post, they did. Where they couldn't, they brought back the contact details with the message drafted, ready to send.

Source: Koki's own case
Use 2

Running a Plumbing Business

A plumbing company owner uses Muse to update jobs, text customers, send Slack updates and remind technicians, so the follow-up work between appointments runs itself.

x.comSource ↗ Use 3

A Form Coach for Your Workouts

A user uploads their own workout footage and has Muse analyze their movements against standard form, then ask how to adjust. Far more useful than another generic workout plan.

x.comSource ↗ Use 4

Chasing an Airline Refund

One user reported getting $200 back from an airline. The agent kept the follow-up going on a schedule, sending reminder emails, and could even make the phone call, the kind of persistence most people do not have the patience for.

x.comSource ↗ Use 5

Finding Scholarships for Your Kid

A parent in the Instinct comments said they used their agent to look for scholarships for their daughter.

Scrubbing Your Data from People-Search Sites

These sites sell your home address, phone number and family connections to anyone. One user had Instinct prepare removal requests across six sites, and clicked the final confirmations themselves.

Use 7

Watching Flight Prices for You

One user has their agent check flight prices on a schedule and alert them when a fare drops to a good level.
