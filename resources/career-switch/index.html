Switching into AI

Break into AI without paying tuition

A curated path for career switchers: paid residencies and fellowships that hire you to learn, founder programs that back people before they have a company, and the free courses with reputations earned over years. Every entry was checked for cost first. Paid bootcamps did not make the list.

Paid residencies & fellowships

Programs that pay you while you switch. Competitive, but they are jobs and research posts, not tuition bills.

Residency · Paid

OpenAI Residency

Six months as a full-time OpenAI employee at about $18,300 a month, embedded in a research team in San Francisco. Built for people coming from math, physics, engineering or self-taught paths, not for career ML researchers.

openai.comApply ↗ Fellowship · Paid

Anthropic Fellows Program

Four months of full-time research with an Anthropic mentor, a weekly stipend of $3,850 and around $15k a month in compute funding. Rolling applications; fellows work in Berkeley or London and aim to publish.

greenhouse.ioApply ↗ Residency · Paid

Apple AIML Residency

A one-year, full-time paid residency inside Apple's AI/ML organization, starting each July. For STEM graduates and engineers switching into ML. Applications usually close the December before.

anitab.orgView ↗ Research apprenticeship · Paid

Cohere Labs Scholars Program

A remote-first, full-time paid research apprenticeship pairing newcomers with Cohere Labs mentors and large-scale experiments. Deliberately open to people who have not published ML papers yet. Applications expected to reopen in fall 2026.

cohere.comView ↗ Apprenticeship · Stipend

AI Singapore AIAP

Singapore's national apprenticeship: 6 or 9 months full-time on real industry projects, a monthly stipend of SGD 4,000, and over 90% of graduates placed in AI roles within six months. Singapore citizens only.

aiap.sgView ↗

Founder routes

If the switch you want is into founding, these back people before companies exist. None charges a fee to apply.

Talent investor

Entrepreneur First

EF selects individuals first: a stipend while you find a co-founder and test ideas, then up to $250K of pre-seed investment when a company forms. Cohorts in London, San Francisco, Bangalore and more. Applying is free.

en.wikipedia.orgRead ↗ Founder fellowship

South Park Commons Founder Fellowship

For people mid-transition who know they want to build but not yet what: $400K upfront for 7%, a guaranteed $600K in the next round, no demo day and no fixed end date. San Francisco, New York, Bangalore.

southparkcommons.comRead ↗ Free course

YC Startup School

Y Combinator's free online school for founders: a roughly seven-week curriculum plus co-founder matching, drawn from the advice YC partners give their own batches. Completely free.

startupschool.orgStart ↗

Free courses worth your time

Self-study routes with reputations earned over years, not marketing. All free; where a paid tier exists, the card says what it actually gates.

Free course

fast.ai · Practical Deep Learning for Coders

The top-down course that turned a generation of programmers into deep learning practitioners. Videos, notebooks and the companion book are all free. Assumes about a year of Python.

course.fast.aiStart ↗ Free course

Hugging Face · LLM & Agents Courses

Hands-on and project-first, completely free, and the Agents Course certificate is free too. The most practical route into AI engineering right now.

huggingface.coStart ↗ Free recordings

Full Stack Deep Learning · LLM Bootcamp

Free recordings of the bootcamp for engineers shipping LLM apps: prompt engineering through deployment and LLMOps, taught by practitioners.

DataTalks.Club Zoomcamps

ML Zoomcamp (4 months), LLM Zoomcamp (10 weeks) and MLOps Zoomcamp: free, open-source, cohort-based courses with an active community and a capstone certificate.

github.comStart ↗ Free · Certificates

Claude Academy · Anthropic

Anthropic's own learning hub: free courses on Claude, Claude Code, the API and MCP, each with a completion badge. The most current material for building on frontier models.

academy.claude.comStart ↗ Free course · In Mandarin

Hung-yi Lee · ML & Generative AI (NTU)

National Taiwan University's Hung-yi Lee teaches machine learning and generative AI in Mandarin, free on YouTube. Widely considered the best Chinese-language ML course anywhere.

youtube.comWatch ↗ Free · Certificate

Elements of AI · University of Helsinki

The introduction for non-experts: no math or programming required, over two million learners, and a free certificate. The gentlest credible on-ramp.

elementsofai.comStart ↗ Free course

Harvard CS50 AI

Harvard's Introduction to Artificial Intelligence with Python. Free to take, with a free CS50 certificate when your projects pass; the paid edX verified certificate is optional.

Kaggle Learn

Short, hands-on micro-courses with certificates: Python, machine learning, deep learning and LLM topics, all runnable in the browser.
