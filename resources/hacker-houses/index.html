SF hacker houses, mapped

Where the city's founders and engineers actually live: communal houses, founder hotels and invite-only mansions. Every entry checked against its official site.

Apply to live there

The classic hacker houses: you apply, residents or a program team pick who gets a room, and the house becomes your network.

Apply · Mission District

Mission Control

San Francisco's oldest hacker house: a community-run commune where residents vote on who moves in. Founders, engineers and builders, mostly long-term stays.

missioncontrol.houseVisit ↗ Apply · Alamo Square

HF0 (Hacker Fellowship Zero)

A 12-week live-in residency for repeat and highly technical founders, based in the Archbishop's Mansion. Housing, meals and day-to-day operations are covered during the batch.

hf0.comApply ↗ Apply · Pacific Heights / Mission

The Residency

Three-month cohorts for deep-tech founders and researchers across several SF mansions: housing, a private chef, coaching and a demo day. The founder track carries a $120K SAFE option.

livetheresidency.comApply ↗ Apply · Russian Hill

908 Coliving

One 16-resident house at 908 Broadway for entrepreneurs, inventors and artists. Furnished rooms, month-to-month terms, breakfast included, and residents are screened by application.

908coliving.comApply ↗ Apply · Lower Haight

Formosa Founders House

A house where Taiwanese founders live and build together, plugged into a network of YC and 500 Global backed housemates. Hot pot dinners are part of the culture.

formosafounders.comApply ↗ Apply · Mission District

20mish (20mission)

A 40-bedroom coliving house running since 2012, started as the city's first crypto hacker house. Private rooms around $1,625 a month, utilities and cleaning included.

20mish.comApply ↗ Apply · Mid-Market / Alamo Square

Accelr8

A founder-run residency operating hacker hotels (1412 on Market, Epik on Polk) plus an Alamo Square mansion, for pre-seed and seed founders staying 30 days or more.

joinaccelr8.comApply ↗ Apply · SoMa

Hive Coliving

A tech-focused founder house at 1040 Folsom: around 50 furnished rooms, a coworking area, month-to-month or four-month leases. Its alumni include the Replit team.

hivecolivingsf.comApply ↗

Just book a bed

No selection process: founder hotels and coliving you can reserve like a hotel, by the night or the month.

Book · Mission / Alamo Square

Foundry

Four San Francisco houses you can simply book: furnished private rooms from $2,290 a month, all-inclusive, one-month minimum. Weekly community and investor dinners, quarterly demo days.

foundry.todayBook ↗ Book · Nob Hill

Superhero Hotel

A 110-room founder hotel at 825 Sutter Street, run by the Frontier Tower team. Book nightly or monthly; the monthly track runs workouts, pitch nights, game nights and hackathons.

superherohotel.comBook ↗

Just outside the city

Two names every SF house hunter runs into, both a short drive south.

HackerHome

Budget coliving houses across Sunnyvale, Mountain View and more of Silicon Valley, bookable on its site or on Airbnb. Family dinners and pitch events; a first landing pad for newcomers and interns.
