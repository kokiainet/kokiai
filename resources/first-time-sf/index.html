Your first Silicon Valley trip, sorted

Get in, get around

No car is normal on a first trip. Trains plus rideshare cover the airport, San Francisco and the Valley towns where most people actually stay.

Airport · San Francisco

SFO (San Francisco International)

The main arrival airport. BART trains and rideshare run straight into the city; the official site has terminal maps, ground transport and flight status.

flysfo.comVisit ↗ Transit · Bay Area

BART

Trains from SFO and Oakland airports into downtown San Francisco and the East Bay. Check schedules and fares here before you land.

bart.govVisit ↗ Transit · SF ↔ Valley

Caltrain

The spine between San Francisco and Silicon Valley: 4th & King to Palo Alto, Mountain View, Sunnyvale and San Jose. Most first stays sit along this line.

caltrain.comVisit ↗ Transit · One card

Clipper Card

One card and app for BART, Caltrain, Muni and buses. Add it to your phone wallet at the airport and stop buying separate tickets.

clippercard.orgVisit ↗ Planning · Bay Area

511 SF Bay

The region's trip planner and live alerts for transit, traffic and ferries. Useful the first time a line changes under you.

511.orgVisit ↗

Phone & money

A US number makes signups, 2FA codes and rideshare accounts much easier. Set the phone up in the first days; move money with an account that shows the real exchange rate.

Phone · Prepaid

Mint Mobile

Prepaid plans you order online and activate on arrival. A common first US number for a stay of a few months.

mintmobile.comVisit ↗ Phone · No contract

Tello

Cheap build-your-own plans with no contract. Good for a light first line, or for keeping a US number after you leave.

tello.comVisit ↗ Money · Multi-currency

Wise

Multi-currency account and international transfers at the mid-market rate. The usual way visitors move money in and out while between countries.

wise.comVisit ↗
